Beverage Station (Coffee & Bar Console)

A custom 1200 mm "Day-to-Night" hospitality console fitted into the corridor south niche. Espresso workflow by morning, illuminated wine and cocktail salon by evening.

1200 mm 60/60 Split Dual-Zone Wine Cooler Oiled Oak & Smoked Glass Blum Legrabox Softclose
Open Schreiner-Dossier (PDF) →

Option A (Warm Oak & Full Drawers): Natural oiled oak finish matching the apartment's OAKO desk, 3 enclosed soft-close drawers, light quartz countertop, dark anthracite tile integration.

Option B (American Walnut & Open Shelf): Rich walnut grain, 2 top drawers with an open display niche below for artisan tea/coffee tins, warm champagne-gold machine accents.

Technical Elevation: 1200 mm width, 600/600 mm split, 900 mm working height, 580 mm machine clearance, 100 mm ventilated plinth.

📐 Functional Module Architecture

Base Cabinet (60 / 60 Split) H: 900 mm · D: 600 mm
  • Left 600 mm Niche: Houses a flush undercounter dual-zone wine cooler (~34–46 bottles). Front-breathing intake via 100 mm plinth ventilation grille.
  • Right 600 mm Drawer System:
    • Top Drawer (130 mm): Solid oak divider for tamping mat, 58mm tamper, WDT tool, scales, sommelier corkscrews.
    • Middle Drawer (240 mm): Fellow Atmos coffee bean canisters, filter papers, tea tins.
    • Bottom Drawer (410 mm): Heavy-duty pullout for tall spirits (whisky, gin, vermouth) and wine decanters.
  • Hardware: Blum Legrabox pure matte black with Blumotion soft-close.
Countertop & Splashback Non-Porous Mineral
  • Material: 20 mm engineered Quartz or Dekton (matte off-white/warm grey, echoing the BoConcept Santiago ceramic table).
  • Durability: Highly resistant to espresso hot oils, acidic red wine spills, and tamping pressure.
  • 150 mm Upstand: Matching stone backsplash protecting the drywall from steam and espresso splashes.
  • Workflow: 55 cm dedicated to espresso machine + burr grinder; 65 cm clear staging area for pouring and decanting.
Smoked Glass Upper Display W: 1200 · H: 450 · D: 320 mm
  • Doors: 2 × hinged doors with ultra-slim matte black anodized aluminium profiles (20 mm) and 4 mm toughened smoked glass (*Rauchglas*).
  • Interior: Adjustable 8 mm glass shelf illuminated by a subtle concealed 2700K warm LED wash.
  • Stemware Rails: 5 rows of brushed brass undermount tracks holding 15–20 inverted wine and champagne glasses.
  • Headroom: Shallow 320 mm depth leaves generous sightlines and headroom when operating the prosumer machine.
Acoustic Slatted Backdrop Floor to Ceiling (~2420 mm)
  • Structure: 27×12 mm natural oiled oak slats mounted on 9 mm black recycled acoustic PET felt.
  • Acoustics: Dampens high-frequency grinder motor vibrations and espresso pump resonance in the open corridor.
  • Hidden Wiring: Allows completely invisible conduit routing for machine quad-outlets and LED drivers without chiseling concrete/drywall.
