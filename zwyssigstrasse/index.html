The 2026/2027 Timing Opportunity: Why Act Now?
The City of Zurich offers an Early Replacement Bonus (~CHF 1,800) specifically for gas heaters younger than 15 years. For Zwyssigstrasse 11 (installed in 2013), this bonus expires in 2027. Furthermore, replacing the system in 2026/2027 allows owners to deduct the entire investment from their cantonal and direct federal income taxes immediately, rather than waiting in limbo until the 2030s.

Technology Spotlight

Passive Free Cooling & Boiler Room Engineering

How geothermal heat pumps deliver superior indoor comfort and fit within our compact basement.

Summer Comfort

Passive Free Cooling

Silent, draft-free, near-zero electricity

At 100–190 meters underground, the earth remains at a steady 8–12°C year-round. During hot summer weeks, cold brine fluid is circulated directly through a heat exchanger into the underfloor heating pipes.

  • Compressor remains off: Only low-wattage circulation pumps run, consuming negligible electricity (~CHF 20–30/summer for the whole building).
  • No draft, no noise: Unlike noisy AC wall splits, cooling is evenly and silently absorbed by the anthracite stone floor tiles.
  • Thermal Ground Regeneration: Heat extracted from the apartments is pumped into the ground, warming the borehole bed and increasing heat pump efficiency for the subsequent winter.
Spatial Integration

Boiler Room Footprint

Solving the tight mechanical room challenge

The existing heating room is compact. Fitting a separate heat pump unit, a domestic hot water (DHW) storage tank, and a heating buffer was flagged by the city coach.

  • Solution 1: Modulating Inverter without Buffer: Underfloor heating concrete screed acts as a massive thermal reservoir. Modern inverter heat pumps dynamically throttle output, eliminating the need for a separate heating buffer.
  • Solution 2: Compact Kombispeicher: A 2-in-1 vertical cylinder that combines DHW hygiene tank with a compact hydraulic separator in a single 80×80 cm footprint.
  • Electrical Grid: ewz confirmed the existing 63A building main can be upgraded to 100A with zero street excavation, only a flat network contribution.
